01 / Introduction

About me

From creative technology to intelligent systems.

My undergraduate studies combined programming, image processing, computer graphics, AI and electronics. As a Vision System Assistant at Hebei Beifang Welding Tools, I developed Python/OpenCV pipelines for weld inspection using real industrial images. In another industrial placement, I investigated false detections in automated visual inspection systems. My research includes vision-guided hand rehabilitation using MediaPipe and a multi-head deep-learning model for edge-computing offloading. I have also led an Arduino-based intelligent umbrella project and contributed to Android and AI-assisted interface design.

Education: MSc Robotics — University of Manchester; BEng Digital Media Technology — Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (First-Class Honours; GPA 3.56/4.0 as listed on my CV).

Selected undergraduate modules: Artificial Intelligence · Computer Graphics · Image Processing · Human-Centric Computing · Data Structures · Advanced Object-Oriented Programming · Signals and Systems · Embedded Computer Systems · Microprocessor Systems.

Research interests & direction.

My current interests include intelligent perception, computer vision, human-centred robotics, edge AI and efficient machine-learning systems. I am pursuing opportunities in robotics and AI research and development, especially where sensing, embedded computing and software engineering meet real-world constraints.
