From pixels to face representations

Two MATLAB labs exploring how local pixel operations improve images, and how eigenface projections turn face images into compact feature vectors.

01 · Problem & goal

Problem & goal

How do local pixel operations change visual quality, and how can a compact linear representation support face matching?

02 · My contribution

My contribution

Implemented neighborhood analysis, resampling and filtering, followed by eigenface projection, reconstruction and distance-based matching in two individual MATLAB labs.

03 · Implementation approach

Implementation approach

Lab 1 implements neighborhood extraction, boundary handling, averaging, nearest-neighbor/bilinear resampling and noise-filtering experiments. Lab 2 projects images into a supplied eigenface basis, reconstructs images from weights, compares distances and implements histogram equalization.

04 · Results & reflection

Results & reflection

The labs show two different kinds of representation: local pixel operations for image quality, and low-dimensional eigenface weights for recognition. Their conclusions depend on the noise, lighting and boundary conditions rather than one universally best method.

Lab 1: the useful conclusion

For salt-and-pepper noise, the report favors a 3 × 3 median filter: it suppresses extreme pixel outliers while retaining more detail than the 5 × 5 window. The larger window smooths more strongly but loses detail. Bilinear interpolation gives smoother scaling than nearest-neighbor in the reported image comparison.

The useful comparison is the balance between noise suppression, edge preservation and detail retention under the tested conditions.

Source: INT302 Lab 1, filtering analysis and conclusions.

Evidence & limits

The reports compare the submitted MATLAB experiments. Resampling and filtering depend on boundary and intensity conventions; face matching is sensitive to noise, lighting and the choice of eigenface components. The browser illustration explains interpolation separately from those experiments.
