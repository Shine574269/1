Image Processing & Eigenfaces

Cleaning and resizing images with local pixel operations, then recognising faces from compact eigenface features.

Role
Individual
Tools
MATLAB
Topics
Filtering · Interpolation · PCA

Challenge

Which local filter removes noise without blurring edges, and how few numbers are needed to recognise a face?

What I did

Implemented neighbourhood filters and nearest-neighbour/bilinear resampling, then eigenface projection, reconstruction, nearest-neighbour matching and histogram equalisation.

Outcome

A 3×3 median filter gave the best noise/detail balance for salt-and-pepper noise. A 100-weight eigenface vector matched the test face, but strong noise and over-exposure broke matching.
