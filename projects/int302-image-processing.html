Image Processing & Eigenfaces

Lab 1 cleans and resizes images pixel by pixel. Lab 2 represents whole faces with eigenfaces and improves contrast.

Two separate individual labs from the same image-processing module. Lab 1 works on individual pixels and their neighbours; Lab 2 works on whole images as vectors.

Lab 1 · Pixel operations

Cleaning and resizing images

  • Pixel neighbourhoods and boundary handling
  • Nearest-neighbour vs bilinear resampling
  • Mean vs median filtering on Gaussian and salt-and-pepper noise

Finding: a 3×3 median filter (PSNR 79.49 dB) removed impulse noise while keeping more detail than 5×5 (77.77 dB).

Lab 2 · Face representation

Eigenfaces and contrast

  • Projecting faces onto 100 eigenfaces and reconstructing them
  • Nearest-neighbour identity matching on the weight vectors
  • Robustness to noise and brightness; CDF-based histogram equalisation

Finding: a 100-number weight vector matched the test face, but strong noise and over-exposure broke matching; equalisation spreads a narrow intensity range across the full scale.
