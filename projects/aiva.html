AIVA — AI Life Steward

A human-centred mobile assistant conceived for scheduling, dietary guidance and everyday decision support in CPT208.

1. Group research poster

The group poster introduced the problem and integrated assistant concept using needs analysis, design alternatives and a prototype plan.

2. Individual presentation and report

Following the group work, the individual materials develop the interface and discuss design refinement, personalisation and privacy considerations.

3. Demonstration video

The individual portrait-format presentation accompanies the design material. The original PPTX is preserved as the canonical slide source to avoid layout changes caused by PDF font substitution.

Focus: Human-centred computing, UI/UX, Figma, iterative prototyping.
