AIVA — AI Life Steward

A human-centred mobile assistant conceived for scheduling, dietary guidance and everyday decision support in CPT208.

01 — Group Poster: Defining the Concept

Our team introduced the AIVA concept through user needs, early design directions and a proposed assistant experience. The poster established the foundation for subsequent individual work.

02 — Individual Design Development

Building on the group concept, I developed the interface design and refined the user experience through a more detailed presentation and report, exploring personalisation, practical workflows and human-centred design choices.

03 — Individual Demonstration

My presentation video walks through the developed concept, interface decisions and intended interactions.

Focus: Human-centred computing, UI/UX, Figma, iterative prototyping.
