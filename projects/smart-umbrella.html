Four-in-One Smart Umbrella

A family-focused smart umbrella prototype designed to improve everyday safety and reduce the difficulty of locating and returning a misplaced umbrella.

The challenge

Conventional umbrellas are easy to misplace and hard to identify after loss. The project also considers safety when families use umbrellas at night or in poor visibility.

Four connected functions

01 · Remote location tracking

A GT-U8 GPS unit and Air780EX 4G module form a long-range location reporting link to a companion mobile interface.

02 · Anti-theft alerts

An MPU6050 motion sensor detects abnormal movement; a mode-switch button and buzzer provide an alert mechanism.

03 · Easier return

An embedded NFC tag enables a finder to access owner-contact information and support return of the umbrella.

04 · Visibility at night

Illuminated trim makes the umbrella's outline easier to see, with steady and warning-light modes described in the competition proposal.

From requirements to a working prototype

01 · Research and selection: The team examined common umbrella-loss scenarios and compared Bluetooth + GPS, Wi-Fi + GPS and 4G + GPS before selecting the long-range 4G approach.

02 · Hardware and design iteration: The prototype integrates an Arduino Uno, GT-U8 GPS, Air780EX 4G module, MPU6050 movement sensor, NFC tag and buzzer. We moved from a folding umbrella to a straight-shaft model for more reliable attachment of the illuminated edge and 3D-printed electronics enclosure.

03 · Testing and refinement: The team tested NFC recognition, movement-triggered alarms, location transmission and illumination. Early sensor noise informed threshold adjustment, while the lighting material was changed to a more flexible EL wire arrangement.

My role in the team

My documented contributions were hardware selection and procurement, NFC information-editing tests, iterations of the illuminated-strip layout, confirmation of the 3D-printed enclosure, video editing and report writing. This project was completed collaboratively as MEC202 coursework and developed into a robotics design competition entry.
