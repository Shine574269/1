Four-in-One Smart Umbrella

An Arduino umbrella that can be located remotely, sounds an alarm when moved, helps a finder return it and lights up after dark.

Stage 1
MEC202 course group project
Stage 2
Competition entry · team leader
Award
Second Prize, Jiangsu Provincial Robot Creativity Competition 2025
Tools
Arduino Uno · MPU6050 · GPS + 4G · NFC · CAD

Challenge

Umbrellas are easy to lose. The device had to locate the umbrella beyond Bluetooth range, detect unwanted movement and fit on the handle.

What I did

Built the first prototype in a course group project, then led a four-person team to refine it for a provincial robot competition: hardware, Arduino firmware, 3D-printed parts and testing.

Outcome

A working prototype that uploads GPS position over 4G, with an accelerometer alarm, NFC owner tag and illuminated edge. Second Prize, 2025.

Stage 1 · Course group project (MEC202, English materials)

A group mechatronics coursework project. My documented contributions: hardware selection and procurement, NFC tag-writing tests, lighting-layout iterations, enclosure confirmation, video editing and report writing.

Stage 2 · Competition entry (Chinese materials)

I then led a team of four to develop the umbrella into an entry for the Jiangsu Provincial College Intelligent Robot Creativity Competition, which won Second Prize (July 2025). As team leader I wrote Arduino C/C++ for NFC data exchange, GPS acquisition and 4G communication, designed structural parts in CAD for 3D printing, and managed task allocation, troubleshooting and the final demonstration.
