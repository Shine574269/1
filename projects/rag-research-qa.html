Retrieval-Augmented QA for Research Papers

Speeding up a research question-answering system with a two-stage hybrid retrieval pipeline.

Before and after

Before

The question-answering system computed similarity against every research abstract one by one, so responses were slow.

After

BM25 quickly filters a keyword candidate set; Sentence-BERT embeddings in a FAISS index then re-rank it semantically. Responses got faster while relevant papers stayed in the top results.

Why two stages?

Lexical search is fast but misses paraphrases; dense embeddings understand meaning but are costly over a whole corpus. Running BM25 first keeps the expensive semantic step small.

Supervisor: Prof. Philipp Koehn, Johns Hopkins University (May – Sep 2023).
