Chest X-ray Enhancement with U-Net

A deep-learning model that restores contrast and sharpness in degraded chest X-rays.

Three ways X-rays degrade

Poor contrastMotion blurInadequate exposure

Each can hide the signs a clinician looks for when screening for pneumonia.

Two design decisions

Standard loss

Treats every image area equally, so the regions that matter most for diagnosis get no special attention.

My weighted loss

Extra weight on anatomical boundaries and suspected abnormal regions, where reconstruction quality matters for diagnosis.

Elastic deformations and intensity variations were added as augmentation so the model copes with different anatomy and exposure conditions.
