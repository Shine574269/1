AIVA — AI Life Steward

01 · The need

Make planning, nutrition and everyday decisions easier through a coherent personal-assistant interface.

02 · My role

Developed an individual interface and design presentation from the team’s AIVA concept, exploring personalization and interaction workflows.

03 · The method

Translate user needs into an information structure, prototype screens and an explanatory walkthrough.

04 · The outcome

An interface-design portfolio and demonstration. This is a design concept, not evidence of a deployed AI assistant.

Design process and contribution

Requirement research (70 valid questionnaires documented by the group), user persona, feature alternatives, low-fidelity wireframes, Figma screen design and six iterative refinements. The proposed app covers diet assistance, personalized decisions and schedules. These are prototype concepts, not verified production features.
