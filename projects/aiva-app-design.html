01 · Problem & goal

Problem & goal

Make planning, nutrition and everyday decisions easier through a coherent personal-assistant interface.

02 · My contribution

My contribution

I was responsible for Figma page implementation and the overall poster framework. My individual presentation develops the group concept into specific screen flows and documents successive interface revisions.

03 · Implementation approach

Implementation approach

Start from everyday planning and decision needs, organize the proposed features, then develop interface prototypes and explain the intended interactions in a design presentation. The group concept and individual design development are identified separately.

Concrete design changes documented in the individual presentation
Initial issueDesign changePurpose
Login and entry screens did not establish the assistant identityAdded a butler-led entry flow and simplified repeated actionsMake onboarding and the assistant’s role clearer
Avatar and wake-word settings were mixed togetherSeparated the customization flow and introduced image-style selectionMake personalization easier to understand
Fixed feature sections offered limited personalizationIntroduced guided questions and configurable sectionsConnect the interface to individual routines
Nutrition advice named nutrients without specific mealsAdded concrete food recommendationsTurn abstract advice into an actionable choice
A separate learning timetable duplicated schedulingOmitted the overlapping pageReduce duplication between features
