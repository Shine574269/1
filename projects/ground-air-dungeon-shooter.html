CPT306 · Game development · Unity / C#

Ground-AirDungeon Shooter

A two-level 2D action shooter built with Unity, combining enemy combat, procedural obstacle placement, player movement, power-ups and a local leaderboard.

01 Goal02 My role03 Method04 Results05 Demo06 Resources

01 · Problem & goal

Problem & goal

Build an action game with two stages that increase challenge through enemy variety, obstacles and pickups.

02 · My contribution

My contribution

The project documents movement, dashing, shooting, health, enemy spawning and game-flow interfaces in Unity/C#.

03 · Implementation approach

Implementation approach

The Unity project connects movement, dash, aim/fire, health, enemy spawning and procedural obstacles. Level 1 introduces basic opponents; Level 2 adds tougher enemy behaviors and denser encounters. Menus, settings, score display and a local leaderboard complete the game loop.

04 · Results & reflection

Results & reflection

The design creates a two-stage combat loop: teach the controls in an open, lower-pressure arena, then increase enemy variety and constrain movement so that cover, dashing and pickups become strategic.

Engineering decisions behind the game feel

Mechanisms and their gameplay purpose
MechanismImplementation describedPlayer-facing effect
DashSpeed 15 versus normal movement speed 3; 1.5-second cooldownA short escape move that requires timing
Obstacle placementOverlap checks with a maximum of 50 retriesVary encounters while reducing overlapping placements
Enemy navigationStuck detection/escape, obstacle avoidance and pursuitKeep opponents moving toward the player through clutter
Attack pickupDamage rises from 1 to 2 for five secondsMake a temporary buff useful against tougher targets

The report identifies occasional navigation problems and an unclear buff-duration indicator. These are concrete polish targets: stronger escape behavior and clearer remaining-time feedback.

Source: Game design specification §§2.3, 3 and 7.

Evidence & limits

The design report notes occasional enemy-navigation issues around obstacles and a power-up UI indicator needing improvement. The supplied executable targets Windows. A WebGL build has not been supplied or verified.

Demonstration & visuals
